Roles in this option

As the fluid-role flow, but with an explicit Consulted party: whoever creates a BC becomes its BC Lead (any business role), and the Lead chooses who to keep informed. The SET BC Lead is always an FP&A person — in every option.

BC Lead → FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local FinanceWhoever sets up the BC becomes its Lead — the mapping is fluid, so any of these business roles can lead a given BC.
BC Contributor → FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local FinancePer-BC exclusivity: the person who set up a BC can't also be a Contributor on it — but the same person can be a Contributor on a different BC.
Consulted → Any business role — chosen by the BC LeadOptional party the BC Lead names at setup to be kept informed. The system notifies only those named, rather than auto-determining who should know.
SET BC Lead → FP&AOversight at SET Area level. Informed of a BC only if the BC Lead adds them as a Consulted person.

About this option

⚙Constraints
  • Fluid role mapping. Within a single BC, the BC Lead can be an FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local Finance person — whoever creates the BC becomes its Lead.
  • Per-BC role exclusivity (not pervasive). A BC's Lead can't be a Contributor on that same BC, but can be a Contributor on a different BC set up by someone else.
  • The BC Lead names the Consulted. At setup the Lead specifies who to keep informed (any business role). The system notifies only those named — it does not try to auto-determine who should know.
  • SET BC Lead is still FP&A. They are informed only if named as a Consulted person on a given BC.
✓Advantages
  • Closer to existing ways of working. The fluid mapping correlates more closely with how teams already operate, and is less rigid.
  • Simpler to implement. An explicit Consulted list replaces a complex set of automatic notification rules — the system just notifies who the Lead chose.
⚠Trade-offs
  • Risk of human error. Because notification is manual, if the BC Lead forgets to add a relevant person — for example the SET BC Lead or an affected FBP — that person simply won't be informed.
