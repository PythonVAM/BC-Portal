Roles in this option

The portal defines three BC-specific roles; each is filled by a generic business role. The SET BC Lead is always an FP&A person — in every option.

SET BC Lead  +  BC Lead → FP&AIn this option the SET BC Lead and BC Lead are treated as one combined role, fulfilled by the same FP&A person.
BC Contributor → FBP  or  GFS Analyst

About this option

⚙Constraints
  • Roles are pervasive and mutually exclusive. A SET BC Lead is only ever a SET BC Lead — never a Contributor — and a Contributor is never a Lead.
  • Contributors are Finance roles. A Contributor is assumed to be either a Finance Business Partner (FBP) or a GFS Analyst.
✓Advantages
  • Full SET Area visibility for the Lead. Because every BC is orchestrated by the SET BC Lead, the Lead reliably sees all of the boundary changes in their SET Area.
  • Simple to implement. Fixed, mutually-exclusive roles keep the IT solution straightforward to build and operate.
⚠Trade-offs
  • FBP sign-off is offline. Where an FBP needs to sign off on a BC, that approval happens as a manual, offline activity — the Lead shares the information with them — unless the FBP was themselves a Contributor on the BC.
