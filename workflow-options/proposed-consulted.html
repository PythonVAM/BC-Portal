Roles in this option

As the proposed workflow, the roles are rigid and pervasive. The only addition is that the SET BC Lead can name Consulted parties to keep informed. The SET BC Lead is always an FP&A person — in every option.

SET BC Lead  +  BC Lead → FP&AThe SET BC Lead and BC Lead are treated as one combined role, fulfilled by the same FP&A person who orchestrates the BC.
BC Contributor → FBP  or  GFS Analyst
Consulted → Any business role — chosen by the SET BC LeadOptional party the Lead names at setup to be kept informed in-system. Notified, but does not act or approve.

About this option

⚙Constraints
  • Roles are pervasive and mutually exclusive. A SET BC Lead is only ever a SET BC Lead — never a Contributor — and a Contributor is never a Lead.
  • Contributors are Finance roles. A Contributor is assumed to be either a Finance Business Partner (FBP) or a GFS Analyst.
  • The Lead names the Consulted. At setup the SET BC Lead specifies who to keep informed (any business role). The system notifies only those named; Consulted is informational, not an approval.
✓Advantages
  • In-system stakeholder visibility. The Lead can keep the right people — for example an FBP who should be aware — informed inside the system, instead of only offline as in the proposed workflow.
  • Full SET Area visibility for the Lead. Every BC is still orchestrated by the SET BC Lead, who reliably sees all BCs in their SET Area.
  • Simple to implement. Fixed roles plus a named-recipient list — no complex automatic notification engine.
⚠Trade-offs
  • Consulted is not an approval. It keeps people informed only. Where a formal FBP sign-off is required, that still happens offline — use the Optional FBP approval option for an in-system gate.
  • Discretionary. It relies on the Lead naming the right people; if someone relevant is omitted, they won't be informed (though the Lead still has full visibility and accountability).

Best-practice assessment
