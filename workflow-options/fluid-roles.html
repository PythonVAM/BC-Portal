Roles in this option

Here the mapping of business roles to BC roles is fluid: whoever creates a BC becomes its BC Lead, and any business role can do so. The SET BC Lead is always an FP&A person — in every option.

BC Lead → FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local FinanceWhoever sets up the BC becomes its Lead — the mapping is fluid, so any of these business roles can lead a given BC.
BC Contributor → FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local FinancePer-BC exclusivity: the person who set up a BC can't also be a Contributor on it — but the same person can be a Contributor on a different BC set up by someone else.
SET BC Lead → FP&AOversight at SET Area level. Not a step in the flow — kept aware of BCs in their area via notification rules.

About this option

⚙Constraints
  • Fluid role mapping. Within a single BC, the BC Lead can be an FP&A, FBP, GFS Analyst or Junior Local Finance person — whoever creates the BC becomes its Lead.
  • Per-BC role exclusivity (not pervasive). A person who sets up a BC (its Lead) can't be a Contributor on that same BC, but can be a Contributor on a different BC set up by someone else.
  • SET BC Lead is still FP&A. They provide SET-Area oversight but are not a mandatory step in the flow.
✓Advantages
  • Closer to existing ways of working. The fluid mapping correlates more closely with how teams already operate.
  • Less rigid. Roles aren't fixed to a person up front — they're assigned per BC, based on who picks it up.
⚠Trade-offs
  • More complex to implement. Fluid, per-BC role assignment is harder to build than fixed, mutually-exclusive roles.
  • Less clear to document. The rules are harder to write down and explain unambiguously.
  • Complex notification rules. Because the SET BC Lead may not be in the flow, a more involved set of notifications is needed to ensure they are always aware of BCs in their area.

Best-practice assessment

This isn't a SOX-controlled process, so controls should be proportionate to risk. Against common governance principles — segregation of duties, four-eyes, auditability and RACI — this option rates:
